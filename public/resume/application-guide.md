# Application and Tailoring Guide

## Default strategy

Use `Adi-Haditya-Nursyam-Software-Engineer-Resume-EN.pdf` for most English-language software applications. Use the matching `-ID.pdf` when the vacancy or employer communicates primarily in Indonesian. Apply the same language rule to the automation and mechanical versions.

Use the automation resume when the vacancy explicitly values embedded software, controls, robotics software, industrial IoT, automation, HMI, telemetry, or physical-system engineering. Use the mechanical resume when the vacancy emphasizes mechanical design, robotics hardware, SOLIDWORKS, FEA/CAE, maintenance, R&D, or prototype manufacturing. Use `Adi-Haditya-Nursyam-General-Master-Resume-{EN|ID}.pdf` when the role is broad, interdisciplinary, management-trainee-like, project/product oriented, or difficult to classify cleanly. The targeted one-page resume remains preferable when the vacancy has a clear discipline.

Salary alone should not override trajectory when the difference is small. Compare monthly gross fixed compensation, not headline compensation that depends on uncertain bonuses.

## Tailoring workflow for each vacancy

1. Save the job description and highlight the five most repeated technical or outcome terms.
2. Choose the correct base resume.
3. Change the professional title only when truthful, such as `Backend-Leaning Full-Stack Engineer` for an API-heavy vacancy.
4. Reorder the skill groups to match the vacancy; never add an unsupported technology.
5. Reorder bullets so the first two answer the employer's most important needs.
6. Keep one differentiating cross-domain bullet only if it helps the role.
7. Choose the language that matches the job advertisement and employer communication. Do not mix section languages within one file.
8. Use the exact filename pattern `Adi-Haditya-Nursyam_<Role>_<Company>_<EN-or-ID>.pdf`.
9. Re-read the final PDF and verify that the company name, role, language, and dates are correct.

## Founder interview answer

Suggested structure:

> I founded Zyx Academy and remain responsible for its product and engineering direction. It is an operating tutoring initiative and a strong source of real product experience, but I am pursuing a full-time software-engineering career. I plan Zyx and tutoring work outside employer hours, and I will respect the employer's intellectual-property, confidentiality, and conflict-of-interest requirements.

Do not volunteer a detailed weekly schedule on the resume. Be ready to explain the boundary clearly in interviews.

## Mechanical-engineering degree answer

Suggested structure:

> Mechanical engineering gave me rigorous systems thinking, modeling, experimentation, and experience with physical constraints. During university, I repeatedly turned organizational and engineering problems into deployed software products. Full-stack software became the work I wanted to pursue professionally, while robotics and automation remain useful differentiators rather than a competing identity.

## Technical interview preparation priorities

1. Data structures and algorithms: arrays, strings, maps, stacks/queues, trees, graphs, recursion, sorting, complexity, and common patterns.
2. Testing: unit, integration, contract, end-to-end boundaries, mocking, and failure-case design.
3. Explain one system deeply: choose Zyx or HMM and practice architecture, tradeoffs, schema, auth, failure modes, deployment, and observability.
4. Rebuild one small API and React interface without AI assistance under time pressure.
5. Prepare SQL exercises covering joins, aggregation, indexing, transactions, and migrations.

## Portfolio route by application

- Software roles: treat Zyx Academy as the flagship production experience and show `zyxacademy.com` directly in its experience heading. Use Me-Bot and SCARA as selected projects because they add different evidence rather than duplicating Zyx.
- Automation roles: link to SCARA, IIoT Conveyor, and PLN evidence.
- Mechanical roles: link to SCARA CAD/robotics evidence and the PLN maintenance/FEA case. Keep software as a differentiator, not the lead story.
- Product engineering roles: lead with Zyx and the founder/product story.
- General graduate, project, R&D, technology operations, or interdisciplinary roles: start from the general master resume, then remove the least relevant section if the employer strongly prefers one page.

## Application checklist

- Resume is one page for ordinary applications.
- Contact information is correct.
- No photo, date of birth, religion, marital status, or full home address.
- Every technology is defensible in an interview.
- Metrics match the fact bank.
- Dates are consistent with LinkedIn.
- PDF opens correctly and text can be selected.
- File name is employer-specific and professional.
- Public links resolve to stable recruiter-facing pages.
- Follow up after an appropriate interval and record the outcome.
